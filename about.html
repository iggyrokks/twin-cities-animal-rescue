Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community nonprofit that brings adopters, foster families, and volunteers together to support animals in need.

We believe small acts of kindness can make a lasting difference in an animal's life.

Our Values

Compassion

We put animal well-being first and treat people with respect.

Trust

We communicate clearly about animal care and responsibilities.

Community

We welcome people with different experiences who want to help.

Our Community Partners

We appreciate the veterinary teams, local businesses, donors, volunteers, and foster families who support rescue work. Their time and resources help animals receive care.
